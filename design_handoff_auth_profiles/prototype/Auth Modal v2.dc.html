3Conta na nuvem — adapted to spec 002 (profiles and accounts)

This modal is now the optional cloud account modal. It opens from an active local profile, or from the profile gate on a new device. There's no Google option. Color identity is picked only when creating a local profile (row 0). In the cloud flows the wheel shows the profile's colors, and on linking, the account's saved colors replace them (FR-026). Every frame works on its own.
