## Irregular openings

Every chaos ability on a plane starts with the **canonical chaos trigger**:

> Sempre que o caos se instaurar, … **(confirm)**

Every encounter ability on a phenomenon starts with the **canonical encounter opening**:

> Quando você encontrar [nome do fenômeno / este fenômeno], …

| English opening (as found in the catalog) | Rendered as |
|---|---|
| "Whenever chaos ensues, …" | Sempre que o caos se instaurar, … |
| "When chaos ensues, …" | Sempre que o caos se instaurar, … |
| "When you encounter [this phenomenon], …" | Quando você encontrar [este fenômeno], … |

A card that uses "this plane"/"this phenomenon" or its own name in the opening keeps that choice
in PT-BR (the name stays in English).

## Prefixes

Ability words and flavor prefixes before the em dash:

| English | PT-BR | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| "Praise Him —" | Praise Him — | proper name/reference, kept in English |
| "Red-Eye —" (Ood Sphere's ability) | Red-Eye — | proper name/reference, kept in English |
| "Song of the Ood —" (Ood Sphere's static text) | Song of the Ood — | proper name/reference, kept in English |
| "Will of the council —" | Vontade do conselho — | official PT-BR ability word |

Rule for new ones: translate when an official PT-BR ability word exists; keep in English when the
prefix is a proper name, quote or reference. For the "starts with" check, look at the text after
the prefix.
