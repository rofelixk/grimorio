1Planar card preview — spec 007 · desktop + mobile
Input decides the surface Hover opens a control-free popover beside the tile (1a). Right-click, Menu / Shift+F10 or a long-press opens the preview dialog: centered with the image beside the text on wide screens (1b), full-screen and stacked on phones (1d).
Reused, not invented Card image frame, ability plate and bead come from the card block and card tile. Popover face = page bg + hairline + the side nav's overlay glow. Dialog = the themed modal's ring + face (no halo or sparks — this is a reading surface).
Try it Every frame is live. Desktop: rest the pointer on a tile, click to toggle, right-click to open. Mobile: press and hold a tile (mouse works too); a plain tap toggles. Esc closes. The draft is shared across frames.

Popover placement (FR-006) Opens to the right of tiles in columns 1–2 and to the left in 3–4, so it never covers its tile; top aligns with the tile and clamps between the top bar and the deck footer. Opens after 300 ms, switches instantly between tiles, closes 150 ms after the pointer leaves tile and popover.
On/off state (FR-003) A bead + micro label ("Ativada no baralho" / "Desativada no baralho") above the name — the same bead as the tile. The image stays at full strength when off. Dialog toggle: secondary "Desativar carta" when on, primary "Ativar carta" when off.
Deviations to confirm Wide dialog text runs at 1rem (not the card block's 1.25rem) so the 880px face holds the full text beside the image. The press-and-hold glow on phones is new: it confirms the hold before the dialog opens. Hint copy for FR-008a is new.
